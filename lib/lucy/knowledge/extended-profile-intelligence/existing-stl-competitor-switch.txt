PROFILE: EXISTING STL OWNER SWITCHING FROM ANOTHER MANAGEMENT COMPANY

    This is the strongest-converting profile we see. The lead already
    runs the property as a short let through a competitor and is
    unhappy with them — they are choosing WHO manages it, not WHETHER
    to do it. They are pre-sold on short-term letting itself, so the
    job is to differentiate Stayful, not to re-sell the model.

    HOW THEY RAISE IT: "I'm already with [competitor] but they're not
    communicating", "my payouts are late or unpredictable", "I'm not
    getting the bookings I expected", "I can never get hold of anyone",
    "I'm thinking about switching management companies".

    VALIDATE: "That's the most common reason owners come to us — when
    you can't get a straight answer or the payouts aren't reliable, it
    wears thin fast. You shouldn't have to chase your own management
    company."

    REFRAME — LEAD WITH THE THREE DIFFERENTIATORS:
    - Direct bookings: around 40 percent of our bookings come direct,
      not through Airbnb or Booking.com — so you're not dependent on
      one platform's ranking the way most companies leave you.
    - Communication: every owner gets their own private Slack channel
      with the team, not a ticketing system or a shared inbox. You ask,
      you get an answer.
    - Payouts: they land between the 1st and 5th every month, and we
      don't miss that window.

    QUANTIFY: "We manage around 80 properties across the UK, payouts
    always land between the 1st and 5th, and that 40 percent direct
    share is unusual in the industry — it's the thing owners notice
    most after they switch."

    THE SWITCH IS EASY: "Switching is straightforward — we coordinate
    the handover, take over the listings, and make sure no booked
    guests are disrupted in the transition. You don't manage any of it."

    OFFER (IF READY THIS MONTH): "If you're ready to come across this
    month and the property's ready to go live, we've got a discounted
    rate of 13 percent plus VAT instead of 15, and it's locked in for
    the life of the contract."

    QUESTION: "What's the main thing your current company is getting
    wrong — is it the communication, the payouts, or the booking
    numbers?" Use their answer to pick which differentiator to lean on.

    BOOK THE WEB MEETING: This profile is hot. Get them onto a call
    with Zac to compare their current numbers against a Stayful
    projection. Status 11, days_until_due 30.

    DO NOT: re-explain what short-term letting is, or how full
    management works — they already know. Going back to basics with a
    switcher reads as not listening and stalls the conversation.
