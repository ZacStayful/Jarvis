LANGUAGE AND TONE — SELLING IN MEANTIME PROFILE

    HOW THEY DESCRIBE THEIR SITUATION:
    "I'm selling the property", "it's on the market", "I just want to make some money while it sits", "the sale is taking longer than expected", "I don't want it sitting empty"

    HOW THEY DESCRIBE THEIR CONCERN:
    "I just need some income in the meantime", "I'm not looking for a long-term thing", "how quickly can you get guests in?"

    TONE:
    Impatient and outcome-focused. Be warm but efficient. Get to the gate question quickly once their situation is clear. Do not slow the conversation down with over-validation.

    VOCABULARY:
    USE: "long-term management partnership" — positions Stayful's offering correctly
    AVOID: "short-term arrangement" — implies Stayful would consider it
    AVOID: Making it sound like STL during a sale is something Stayful normally does

    MAYBE LEAD — POST MEETING FOLLOW-UP:
    A MAYBE lead agreed to the web meeting to see the numbers. After the meeting they may go quiet. This is the most likely outcome.

    DAY 1: "Hi [name], hope the call with Zac was useful. Did anything from the numbers change how you're thinking about it?"

    DAY 3 if no response: "Just checking in — I know you were weighing up whether the income would change your thinking on the sale. Is there anything from the call you wanted to go over?"

    DAY 7 final: "Hi [name] — if the numbers made sense and you're thinking about keeping the property, Zac would love to have another conversation. If the sale is still the plan, completely understand — no pressure either way."

    AFTER 3 TOUCHPOINTS WITH NO RESPONSE:
    Status: Abandoned (2), days_until_due 0, lost_reason: decided_to_sell
