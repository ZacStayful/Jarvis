BUDGET TIER 3 — £250,000 TO £400,000

    Mid to premium tier. Lower gross yield percentages but stronger average daily rates and premium guest profiles. More resilient during downturns. Some markets generate exceptional peak revenue that compensates for softer shoulders.

    EDINBURGH
    Average property price: £270,000 to £320,000 (city centre higher)
    Best STL neighbourhoods: Old Town, New Town, Marchmont, Stockbridge
    STL monthly revenue (2-bed excluding August): £1,800 to £3,200 per month
    Peak August revenue (Festival Fringe): £4,000 to £8,000+ per month
    CRITICAL REGULATORY NOTE: Short-term let licensing is required in Edinburgh. Existing permitted properties command a premium. Always verify a property has STL planning consent or is in a permitted zone BEFORE recommending purchase. This must be communicated clearly to any lead considering Edinburgh.
    Why it works: Edinburgh Festival Fringe generates 3 million guest nights in August alone; demand from international tourism, universities, and corporate travel runs year-round; properties with existing consent are rare and create strong barrier to entry for competitors
    Stayful serviceability: NOT in footprint — refer out

    YORK
    Average property price: approximately £306,000 city average — entry possible from £250,000
    Best STL areas: city centre, Micklegate, The Mount, Shambles area
    STL monthly revenue (2-bed): £1,600 to £2,800 per month
    Estimated gross STL yield: 6 to 8%
    Key demand drivers: 8.9 million visitors annually generating £1.7 billion for the local economy; year-round heritage tourism (Jorvik, Shambles, York Minster) — no seasonal cliff in winter
    Why it works: one of the most consistent year-round STL performers in the UK; demand does not collapse in winter because heritage tourism runs 12 months; short-term rentals in York often earn double long-term rental income
    Stayful serviceability: NOT in footprint — refer out

    BIRMINGHAM
    Average property price: £208,000 to £240,000 (city centre higher)
    Best STL areas: B1/B3 (city centre and Jewellery Quarter), B15 (Edgbaston), B5 (Southside)
    STL monthly revenue (2-bed): £1,400 to £2,600 per month
    Estimated gross STL yield: approximately 7%
    Key demand drivers: UK's second city, NEC (UK's largest exhibition centre), HS2 arriving 2026-27 cutting London journey to 38 minutes, Commonwealth Games legacy venues, Queen Elizabeth Hospital medical tourism
    Why it works: NEC drives consistent conference and exhibition demand throughout the year — reduces seasonality risk significantly; HS2 represents a long-term property value catalyst
