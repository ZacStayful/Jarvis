HOW STAYFUL SOURCES CLEANERS AND MAINTENANCE

    Q: How do you find cleaners? / Who does the cleaning? / How do you handle maintenance? / Do you have your own cleaners? / Who do you use for repairs?

    A: Stayful works on an approved framework of trusted suppliers. When a new property is coming onboard, Stayful advertises the property to its closest approved suppliers in that area. The supplier who can provide the best standard of service for the best price is awarded the project, subject to a property inspection once the landlord is signed up and onboarding begins.

    KEY POINTS FOR LUCY TO COMMUNICATE:
    - Stayful does not use random contractors — all suppliers are part of an approved framework
    - When a new property comes onboard, it is actively advertised to trusted local suppliers
    - The selection is competitive — best service for best price, not just cheapest
    - The property is inspected before the supplier is confirmed, so the right standard is set from the start
    - This applies to both cleaning and maintenance

    If a lead is concerned about the quality of cleaning or maintenance given Stayful is not geographically local: this framework is exactly how Stayful maintains quality across different areas. The local network is established, vetted, and competitive — not ad hoc.

    IMPORTANT CONTEXT: This answer directly addresses the local presence objection for leads who worry Stayful cannot manage their property well from a distance. The approved supplier framework is part of how Stayful delivers a consistent standard regardless of location.
