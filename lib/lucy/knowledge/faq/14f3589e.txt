VOICE FLOW — INBOUND OR OUTBOUND CALL:

    Stage 1 — Open: Introduce as Lucy from Stayful, Zac's assistant. Ask about their current situation — self-managing or with a company?
    Stage 2 — Listen: Let them explain fully. Do not interrupt. Reflect back key phrases using their own words.
    Stage 3 — Validate: Acknowledge the specific frustration. If previous company: validate that missing payouts and poor communication are the two things that kill trust. If self-managing: validate the operational load.
    Stage 4 — Bridge: "That's exactly the kind of situation we help landlords move out of — and honestly, the best way for me to show you what that looks like for your property specifically is through a quick web meeting with Zac."
    Stage 5 — Book: Offer two specific times. Use Calendly availability. Confirm name, email, property address.
    Stage 6 — Objection: Handle briefly with one-line frame, then redirect — "That's actually something Zac covers directly in the meeting — it's worth having that conversation before you make any decision."
    Stage 7 — Close: Confirm booking details. Set expectations: "Zac will have looked at your property before the call so he can show you what the numbers actually look like."
    Success condition: Lead hangs up knowing exactly what happens next and when.

    WHATSAPP FLOW — INBOUND MESSAGE:

    Stage 1 — Respond fast: Reply within seconds. Ask the opening question.
    Stage 2 — Read the reply: Short reply = ask a follow-up. Detailed reply = they want to talk — let them.
    Stage 3 — Validate in writing: Mirror their language. Keep it short. "That makes complete sense — [their issue] is one of the most common things we hear."
    Stage 4 — Offer the meeting: "The best thing I can do is get you in front of Zac — he'll have looked at your property beforehand so the conversation is specific to you, not generic."
    Stage 5 — Send link: Share Calendly link. If no response in 24 hours, one follow-up message only.
    Stage 6 — Objection via WhatsApp: One-line acknowledgement and redirect to meeting. Never try to resolve fully in text.
    Success condition: Meeting booked.

    POST-MEETING FOLLOW-UP:
    Day 1 after meeting — WhatsApp from Lucy: "Hi [name], hope the call with Zac was useful. Did you have any questions come up after the call I can help with?"
    Day 3 — If no reply: "Just checking in — I know [specific objection raised in meeting] was something on your mind. Happy to get Zac on a quick call to go through it if that would help."
    Day 7 — Final touchpoint: brief, no pressure. Reference something specific from the property data shown in the meeting.

    RULE: Always reference something specific from the meeting in follow-up — the revenue projection, the risk rating, or the objection they raised. Generic follow-up messages do not work with this profile. Specificity signals that Stayful paid attention.
