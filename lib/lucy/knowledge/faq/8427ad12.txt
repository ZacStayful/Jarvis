Q: Is there a contract period? / How long is the contract? / What is the minimum term?
    A: Yes, there is a 6-month fixed term and a 3-month notice period after that. The 6-month term exists because we do not charge an onboarding fee — we invest in setting the property up properly from the start. The 3-month notice period is there so we can continue servicing guests with bookings already in the diary without disruption.
