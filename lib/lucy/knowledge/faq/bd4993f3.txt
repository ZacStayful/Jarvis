PROFILE: Rent to Rent — Seeking Management Company
    TIER: Tier 3 — Low to No Conversion (viability unknown until web meeting)
    AGENT TREATMENT: Empathetic and efficient — identify quickly, answer concerns briefly, book the web meeting. Do not pre-judge viability. The web meeting is where Stayful assesses whether the margin works.

    WHO THEY ARE:
    - Has taken on a rent-to-rent property and is sub-letting it on a short-term basis
    - Almost always their first time in the short-term letting industry — jumped in without proper training or preparation
    - Quickly realised they are not making the money they expected and feel out of their depth
    - Coming to Stayful to offload the stress of managing it themselves
    - Not an experienced investor — an overwhelmed individual who moved fast and is now struggling

    EMOTIONAL STATE ON ARRIVAL:
    Stressed and overwhelmed. They feel out of their depth and are not making the income they need. There may be embarrassment about having jumped in without preparation. They want someone to take this off their hands.

    WHAT THEY WANT:
    - To hand over the day-to-day management to someone who knows what they are doing
    - To stop the operational stress
    - Ideally to start making the money they thought they would make

    HOW LUCY IDENTIFIES THEM:
    This usually becomes clear naturally during the conversation — the lead will mention they are renting a property and sub-letting it, or it will emerge when Lucy asks about their current situation. Lucy does not need to interrogate. Let the lead explain and it will surface.

    VIABILITY:
    Whether a rent-to-rent arrangement is viable with Stayful depends on the specific numbers — the rent they are paying the landlord, the expected STL revenue, and Stayful's 15% + VAT management fee. This cannot be assessed on the first call. The web meeting is the right place to go through this. Lucy does not pre-judge or rule this lead out on the call.

    LUCY'S SINGLE OBJECTIVE WITH THIS PROFILE:
    Listen, show understanding, briefly address any concern raised, and book the web meeting. The web meeting is the answer to every question.
