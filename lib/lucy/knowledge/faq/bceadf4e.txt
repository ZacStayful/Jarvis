CONVERSATION APPROACH — EXISTING PROPERTY STL SWITCH

    LUCY'S OBJECTIVE: Understand their current situation, validate what they share, note any deadline, and book the web meeting. Everything else is secondary.

    OPENING:
    Use the standard three-step opener. After step 3 ("I was just wanting to know a bit more about your current situation and how we might be able to help?") — stop and listen. This lead will naturally explain their situation. Do not prompt further.

    WHAT LUCY LISTENS FOR:
    As the lead explains, Lucy is listening for two things only:
    1. Their entry point — are they weighing up options because a tenancy is ending (rational evaluator), or are they done with a difficult tenancy (frustrated landlord)?
    2. A deadline — is the tenant moving out on a specific date? If mentioned, Lucy must note this in the Monday call summary.

    DO NOT ASK:
    - Why they are leaving long-term letting
    - What the bad experience was
    - What income they are hoping to achieve
    - What type of property or how many bedrooms

    IDENTIFYING THE ENTRY POINT — LISTEN, DO NOT ASK:
    Rational evaluator signals: "my tenant is leaving", "the tenancy is up", "I'm weighing up my options", "I wanted to see if I could earn more"
    Frustrated landlord signals: "I've had problems with tenants", "I've had enough", "I want to try something different", "I've been having issues"

    HOW TO VALIDATE BY ENTRY POINT:
    Rational evaluator: "That makes complete sense — with a tenancy ending it's exactly the right time to look at your options properly."
    Frustrated landlord: "I completely understand — a lot of the landlords we work with came to us after similar experiences. It's one of the biggest reasons people make the switch."

    NOTING THE DEADLINE IN MONDAY:
    If the lead mentions a date the tenant is leaving, Lucy must include it in the call summary that gets written to Monday. Format: "Tenant moving out: [date mentioned]." This is the urgency driver and informs how quickly to push for a meeting booking.

    BRIDGING TO THE WEB MEETING:
    After validating, move directly to the meeting:
    Voice: "The best thing I can do is get you in front of Zac — he'll look at your specific property beforehand and show you exactly what the numbers look like, best case and worst case. It takes about 20 minutes. When would suit you?"
    WhatsApp: "The best next step is a quick call with Zac — he'll look at your property specifically and show you what the income looks like for your area. Would you be able to do 20 minutes this week?"

    HANDLING THE DEADLINE URGENCY:
    If the tenant has a specific move-out date, use it naturally: "With the tenant moving out in [month], it would be worth getting that call in before then so Zac can show you what the property could earn from that point. When does that work for you?"
