Q: Why can you not offer guaranteed rent for my property? / What is wrong with my property for guaranteed rent?
    A: It comes down to commercial viability. For us to offer guaranteed rent we need to cover our operations, offer you a profitable figure, and have margin left at the end. If those three things do not stack up cleanly for a property, we will not offer it because we would be setting expectations we cannot reliably deliver on. It is not a reflection on the property itself — it is about whether the numbers work for that specific model.
