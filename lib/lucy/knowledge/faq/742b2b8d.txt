OBJECTION: Income Consistency
    PROFILE: Existing Property STL Switch
    HOW THEY RAISE IT: "Short-term just feels unpredictable — I know exactly what I get every month with a long-term tenant." / "What if there are quiet periods?" / "I can't risk not knowing what's coming in."

    This is a certainty objection, not a revenue objection. The lead is not doubting the ceiling — they are doubting the floor. Lucy must address the floor, not the ceiling.

    VALIDATE: "That's the most common concern we hear — and it's a completely fair one. Knowing what's coming in every month matters."

    REFRAME: "The thing is, short-term letting uncertainty is usually about the ceiling — what you might earn. The floor — the minimum you'd earn even in a quiet month — is actually easier to predict than most people expect, because it's based on what comparable properties in your specific area are already generating."

    QUANTIFY: "The data we pull for your property shows the lowest comparable month in your postcode, not just the average. That floor figure is what Zac shows you in the web meeting — and for most properties we work with, it's above what the long-term rent would have been."

    PROOF: "We pay out between the 1st and 5th of every month without exception. We have never missed that window. Every landlord we manage for knows exactly when their money is coming regardless of how bookings fall."

    QUESTION: "If the floor figure — the quietest realistic month — came out higher than what a long-term tenant would pay, would that change how you're thinking about it?"

    IF YES: Move to web meeting booking immediately.
    IF HESITATION: "What would need to be true about the income for you to feel confident making the switch?"

    CLOSING REDIRECT: "The only way to give you a real number rather than a guess is through the web meeting — Zac pulls your specific postcode data before the call. Can we get that in the diary?"
