PROFILE: Existing STL — Seeking Management Company
    TIER: Tier 1 — Highest Conversion
    AGENT TREATMENT: VIP Fast Track — do not over-qualify, move directly to web meeting

    WHO THEY ARE:
    - Already operating a short-term let — either self-managing or previously with a management company that failed them
    - Remote landlord — cannot be hands-on with day-to-day operations
    - Typically 1–3 properties; portfolio landlords also appear in this segment
    - Has already decided they need a management company — they are choosing who, not whether
    - Arrives with formed opinions about what good management looks like: communication and revenue

    TWO ENTRY POINTS — SAME PROFILE:
    Entry Point A — Frustrated with previous management company: Communication failures, missed or late payouts, bookings drying up. They trusted a company and feel let down. They want reliability above everything.
    Entry Point B — Burned out from self-managing: Guest handling, day-to-day issues, and operational load have become unsustainable. They want it completely off their plate.

    EMOTIONAL STATE ON ARRIVAL:
    Frustrated from a bad experience or from being overwhelmed. Cautious but decisive — they have already decided to hand off management. They will measure Stayful against what they know good management should look like.

    WHAT THEY WANT:
    - Primary desire: fully hands-off management — "just want it off my plate"
    - Revenue expectation: long-term stable revenue, not a ceiling promise
    - Communication standard: always knowing what is happening without having to chase
    - First question: will this property be profitable and can you deliver long-term hassle-free bookings

    DECISION TIMELINE: Fast — typically within 10 days of first contact.
