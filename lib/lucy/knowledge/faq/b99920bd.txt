HOW STAYFUL MANAGES BOOKINGS

    Q: How do the bookings get managed? / Where do the bookings go? / Do I have access to the bookings? / How does the booking system work?

    A: All bookings come in on Stayful's account and channel manager. As the landlord, you have visibility of the bookings through the channel manager access we provide. Payouts for bookings are distributed between the 1st and 5th of every month. With each payout, Stayful provides a full statement so you have a clear breakdown of income and any deductions for that month.

    KEY POINTS FOR LUCY TO COMMUNICATE:
    - Bookings are managed on Stayful's account and channel manager — not the landlord's personal Airbnb account
    - The landlord has access through the channel manager so they can see what is booked
    - Payouts happen between the 1st and 5th of every month — Stayful has never missed this window
    - A statement is provided with every payout showing the full breakdown
    - This is a fully managed system — the landlord does not need to log in and manage anything themselves

    If a lead asks about having bookings go directly to their own Airbnb account or receiving income directly from the platforms: this is not how Stayful operates. Stayful manages everything on its own account and channels, then pays out the landlord's share monthly with a full statement. This is standard practice for professional management companies and is what enables Stayful to optimise pricing and occupancy across the portfolio.
