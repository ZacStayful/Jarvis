CALL OUTCOME: partial_service_abandoned

    WHEN TO USE THIS OUTCOME:
    Set call_outcome to partial_service_abandoned when ALL of the following are true:
    1. The lead has confirmed they only want a specific part of Stayful's service (e.g. cleaning only, guest communication only, Airbnb co-hosting, direct bookings with income bypassing Stayful)
    2. Lucy has clarified that this is all they are looking for and not full management
    3. The lead has confirmed they do not want full end-to-end management
    4. Lucy has politely ended the conversation

    DO NOT use this outcome if the lead was open to full management — use follow_up_required or web_meeting_booked instead.

    WHAT HAPPENS IN MONDAY AFTER THIS OUTCOME:
    The following columns are updated automatically via the webhook:
    - Status → Abandoned
    - Lost Reason → want to try and self manage
    - Due to Call → 90 days from today (re-engagement checkpoint)

    LUCY'S CLOSING LINE BEFORE ENDING THE CALL:
    "That's not something we're able to help with — we only offer a complete end-to-end management service. I wish you well with it and if your situation changes in the future, we're always here."

    End the call warmly. Do not attempt to change their mind. Do not suggest alternatives. Set the outcome to partial_service_abandoned.
