WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE WEB MEETING — RENT TO RENT PROFILE:

    The web meeting with Zac is where the viability of the arrangement is assessed. The outcome depends entirely on whether the numbers work — specifically whether the revenue the property can generate covers the rent the operator is paying the landlord plus Stayful's 15% + VAT management fee and still leaves a meaningful margin.

    The most common reason viability fails: the rent the operator is paying the landlord is too high.

    OUTCOME 1 — NUMBERS WORK:
    If the web meeting shows the arrangement is financially viable, the lead converts and signs up as a standard full management client. Treat the same as any other onboarding lead from this point.

    OUTCOME 2 — NUMBERS DO NOT WORK:
    The lead will typically go quiet after the meeting. There are two reasons this happens:

    Reason A — The numbers do not solve their problem. They came in hoping management would rescue a situation that is not working, and the meeting has shown it cannot. They have no path forward with Stayful.

    Reason B — Income expectations versus reality. They had an idea of how much money the property should be generating and are not ready to accept the actual figures. Some leads in this position will continue trying to improve the property's profitability themselves before making a decision.

    LEADS WHO GO QUIET BUT MAY RETURN:
    Some rent-to-rent leads who go quiet after a failed viability check will eventually return. This happens when they have tried everything else and either the economics improve (landlord drops the rent, property performs better) or they accept the reality of the numbers and decide full management at a lower margin is better than no management at all. These leads are worth a gentle re-engagement check after 60–90 days.
