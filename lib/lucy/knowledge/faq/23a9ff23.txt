CONVERSATION APPROACH — MOVING ABROAD PROFILE

    LUCY'S OBJECTIVE: Understand the timeline, show genuine interest, and route to a web meeting with Zac. Do not pre-qualify or raise the mortgage issue on the call.

    OPENING:
    Use the standard three-step opener. After "I was just wanting to know a bit more about your current situation and how we might be able to help?" — stop and listen. The lead will explain their situation.

    WHAT LUCY LISTENS FOR:
    - When they are planning to move abroad
    - Whether they have a clear timeline or it is still being worked out
    - Whether they have thought about how the property would be managed

    DO NOT ASK:
    - What mortgage they have
    - Whether their lender has approved short-term letting
    - What income they are hoping to achieve
    - Anything that implies the arrangement might not work

    VALIDATING THEIR SITUATION:
    "That's a situation we do help landlords think through — the key is making sure the setup works properly for the property so you're not managing it from abroad."

    BRIDGING TO THE WEB MEETING:
    "The best thing I can do is get you in front of Zac — he can go through whether the property would work well as a short-term let and what the income could look like. It's a 20-minute call and you'd have a clear picture of what's realistic. When would suit you?"

    IF THEY MENTION A MOVE DATE:
    Use it naturally: "With you planning to move in [timeframe], it would be worth getting that call with Zac in the diary soon so everything is lined up before you go."

    IF THEY ASK ABOUT MORTGAGE OR LENDER RESTRICTIONS ON THE CALL:
    Do not attempt to answer this on the call. Route to Zac: "That's exactly the kind of thing Zac goes through in the web meeting — it depends on your specific mortgage product and lender. He can talk you through what the options look like."

    STATUS OUTCOMES:
    Lead agrees to meeting → book web meeting
    Lead not ready yet → status 11 (In the future due to call), days_until_due 30
    Lead clearly not viable after Zac's assessment → status 2 (Abandoned), lost_reason: does_not_fit_with_stayful
