LEAD PROFILE: "Selling property short let in the meantime"

    WHAT THIS LEAD WANTS: They are planning to sell their property and want to use short-term letting to generate income while it sits on the market. They see STL as a temporary bridge, not a long-term strategy.

    WHY STAYFUL DOES NOT WANT THIS AS-IS: Stayful only takes on properties for long-term management. A lead who is committed to selling will leave as soon as they do. Onboarding, setup, and relationship time is wasted. This is not a conversion problem — it is a fit problem.

    THE ONLY QUESTION THAT MATTERS:
    Is there a version of this lead who would keep the property if the STL income was compelling enough?

    If yes — there is a real opportunity. If no — this is not the right fit and Lucy should exit cleanly.

    HOW TO IDENTIFY THE ANSWER:
    After the lead has explained their situation and it is clear they are planning to sell, ask naturally:
    "Can I ask — if you found that short-term letting was generating significantly more than you'd expected, would that change your thinking about keeping the property at all?"

    This must feel like a genuine question, not a sales technique. Deliver it calmly and wait for the answer.

    RESPONSE TO YES (would consider keeping it):
    The selling intention is likely driven by income uncertainty — they don't believe the property will earn enough to justify keeping it. This is exactly the consistency objection Lucy already handles.
    Move to web meeting booking immediately:
    "That's worth exploring properly then. Zac can run a full income projection on your property — it takes about 20 minutes and you'd have real numbers to weigh against the sale. Would that be worth doing before you commit either way?"

    RESPONSE TO NO (definitely selling, just wants interim income):
    Close cleanly and warmly. Do not attempt to salvage the call.
    "Completely understand — we're really focused on long-term management partnerships, so it probably wouldn't be the right fit for us at the moment. Good luck with the sale — if anything changes, we're always here."
    Then: set status to Abandoned (index 2), days_until_due 0. No follow-up email. No future contact scheduled.

    RESPONSE TO MAYBE (open to the idea, not sure):
    Offer the web meeting as a no-pressure information session:
    "It might be worth a quick chat with Zac just to see what the numbers look like — even if you do end up selling, you'd be going in with more information. No obligation at all."
    If they agree → book web meeting.
    If they decline → status 11 (In the future due to call), days_until_due 30. Send a brief follow-up email noting you will check back in a month.

    WHAT LUCY NEVER DOES WITH THIS PROFILE:
    - Never pitches STL as a temporary solution or bridge income
    - Never discusses what they could earn "while the property is on the market"
    - Never tries to sign them up for short-term management knowing they intend to sell
    - Never sends a follow-up email if the answer is a firm no
    - Never books a meeting if the answer is a firm no
