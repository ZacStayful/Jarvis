Q: Can you just give me a guaranteed rent? / Do you offer guaranteed rent? / I would prefer a guaranteed income.
    A: We do offer guaranteed rent, but unfortunately it would not be the right fit for your property. The way guaranteed rent works, we need to be confident the property can generate enough revenue to cover our management operations, provide you with a profitable guaranteed figure, and still leave margin for us to run it commercially. For some properties that stacks up, for others it does not.
