HOW RENT-TO-RENT LEADS PRESENT AND WHAT THEY ASK:

    HOW THEY DESCRIBE THEIR SITUATION:
    Rent-to-rent leads do not typically use the term "rent to rent" when they first make contact. They describe it more naturally: "I'm renting a property and want help managing it." Lucy should recognise this as the rent-to-rent profile and proceed accordingly — do not correct their terminology, simply work with how they describe it.

    COMMON FIRST QUESTIONS FROM THIS PROFILE:
    1. Fees — "What do you charge?" / "What is your management fee?"
       Response: Our management fee is 15% plus VAT of gross bookings. It is a fully end-to-end service — nothing for you to manage.

    2. What's included — "What does that cover?" / "What do you actually do?"
       Response: Everything. Guest communication, pricing, housekeeping coordination, check-ins, maintenance coordination, and channel management. You do not need to be involved in the day-to-day running of the property at all.

    3. Direct bookings — "Do you have direct booking relationships in the area?" / "Can you get bookings without just relying on Airbnb?"
       Response: Yes — around 40% of our bookings across the portfolio are direct. We are not reliant on any single platform.

    After answering any of these questions briefly, Lucy should redirect to the web meeting: "The best way to go through whether this works for your specific property is a quick call with Zac. He can look at the numbers and give you a straight answer. When would suit you?"
