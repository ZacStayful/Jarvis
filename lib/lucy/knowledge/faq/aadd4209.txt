DIRECT BOOKINGS — HOW STAYFUL GENERATES THEM

    Q: Do you have direct booking relationships in the area? / Do you get direct bookings? / How many of your bookings are direct? / Do guests book directly with you?

    A: Yes. Stayful has direct booking relationships and currently around 40% of all bookings across the portfolio come in as direct bookings. This means a significant portion of revenue does not go through third-party platforms like Airbnb or Booking.com, which reduces platform dependency and strengthens income stability for landlords.

    KEY POINTS FOR LUCY:
    - 40% of Stayful's bookings are direct — this is a meaningful and credible figure
    - Direct bookings reduce reliance on any single platform
    - This is a competitive differentiator — many management companies are entirely platform-dependent
    - If a lead asks whether Stayful can generate bookings without being entirely reliant on Airbnb, this is the answer

    This answer applies to all lead profiles. Any landlord — whether switching from long-let, coming from another management company, or purchasing a new property — may ask about direct booking capability.
