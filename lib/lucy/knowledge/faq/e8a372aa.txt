QUALIFICATION GATE — MOVING ABROAD PROFILE

    This is the single most important instruction for this profile. Lucy must establish the lead's time away before booking any web meeting.

    THE RULE:
    If the lead is planning to be away from the UK for a minimum of 6 months per year — they are a qualified lead. Book the web meeting.
    If the lead is planning to be away for less than 6 months per year — they are not a qualified lead. Do not book the web meeting. Exit the conversation cleanly.

    HOW TO ESTABLISH THIS NATURALLY:
    Do not ask "how long will you be away?" as a blunt qualification question. Work it in naturally after they have explained their situation:
    "Just so I can point you in the right direction — roughly how long are you planning to be out of the UK? Are we talking a few months or more of a long-term move?"

    If they say 6 months or more per year → qualified. Proceed to web meeting booking.
    If they say a few weeks, a couple of months, or anything under 6 months → not qualified. Exit cleanly.

    EXIT SCRIPT — NOT AWAY LONG ENOUGH:
    "That's really helpful to know — to be honest, for the arrangement to work well financially for you, we'd typically need to be managing the property for at least six months of the year. Below that it becomes difficult to generate the kind of returns that make it worthwhile for you. It's probably not the right fit for us at this point."

    Keep in touch ask: "Would it be okay if I kept your details? If your plans change and you end up being away for longer, it would be worth picking up the conversation then."
    If yes: "Brilliant — I'll make a note. Good luck with the plans."
    If no: "No problem at all — best of luck."

    EXIT SCRIPT — LEAD DECIDES NOT TO MOVE ABROAD:
    If a lead who was previously engaged confirms they are no longer planning to move abroad:
    "That completely makes sense — if the move is off the table then the short-let arrangement obviously falls away with it. If things change and you do end up moving, we'd love to have another conversation."
    No keep in touch ask needed unless they bring up future plans. Exit cleanly.

    PEAK SEASON NOTE:
    If the lead mentions they will be away during summer or other peak STL periods only — this significantly impacts income potential. Do not raise this concern on the first call.
