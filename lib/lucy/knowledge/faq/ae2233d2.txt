EXIT SCRIPT — SELLING IN MEANTIME PROFILE (FIRM NO)

    This supplements the existing selling profile source. When the lead confirms they are definitely selling and have no openness to keeping the property, use this exit script exactly.

    REASON TO GIVE:
    "The reason we wouldn't be the right fit is that we only take on properties for long-term management — offering a short-term arrangement dilutes the service we can deliver and we wouldn't be able to guarantee the same standard we're known for."

    FULL EXIT SCRIPT — FIRM NO:
    "Completely understand — the reason we wouldn't be the right fit is that we're focused on long-term management partnerships, and taking on a property short-term dilutes the service we can deliver. We wouldn't be able to guarantee the standard we're known for in that arrangement. I hope the sale goes smoothly — if anything changes and you do decide to keep it, we'd love to have that conversation."

    KEEP IN TOUCH ASK — ALWAYS END WITH THIS ON A FIRM NO:
    "Would it be okay if I kept your details on file? Just in case the situation changes down the line."

    If they say yes: "Great — I'll make a note. Best of luck with the sale."
    If they say no: "No problem at all — best of luck with it."

    TONE: Warm, honest, and clean. No attempt to change their mind. No hard sell. Leave the door open without pressure.

    LOST REASON — CRITICAL RULE:
    Only set lost_reason to decided_to_sell when the lead gives a firm NO with zero openness — they are definitely selling, have no interest in keeping the property under any circumstances, and the conversation is being closed.

    Do NOT set decided_to_sell if:
    - The lead said maybe or showed any hesitation
    - The lead agreed to a web meeting
    - The lead asked to think about it

    For MAYBE outcomes: no lost_reason is set. Status moves to In the future due to call (11), days_until_due 30. The lead is not lost — they are undecided.

    MONDAY STATUS:
    Firm NO → status Abandoned (2), days_until_due 0, lost_reason: decided_to_sell
    MAYBE → status In the future due to call (11), days_until_due 30, no lost_reason
    YES → book web meeting, no status update needed
