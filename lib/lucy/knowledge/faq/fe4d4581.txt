OBJECTION: Management Effectiveness
    PROFILE: Existing Property STL Switch
    HOW THEY RAISE IT: "How do I know you'll actually manage it properly?" / "What makes you better than doing it myself?" / "I've heard mixed things about short-term letting management companies." / "How do I know the property will be well looked after?"

    This is the easiest objection to handle for this profile. The lead is not saying no — they are asking for proof of quality. Give it to them specifically and briefly, then move to the meeting.

    VALIDATE: "That's exactly the right question to ask — management quality is everything with short-term letting. The difference between a well-managed and a poorly-managed property can be thousands of pounds a year."

    REFRAME: "The best way to answer that isn't with a general claim — it's with the numbers from properties we already manage near yours. Occupancy rates, revenue figures, review scores. That's real proof, not a promise."

    QUANTIFY: "Across our portfolio, we're averaging around 40% direct bookings — so we're not dependent on Airbnb alone. That's a meaningful indicator of how well the properties are performing and how we manage guest relationships."

    PROOF: "Zac will show you specific nearby properties in the web meeting — same property type, similar area, real numbers. That answers the management quality question better than anything I can say on this call."

    QUESTION: "Would seeing how properties near yours are performing give you more confidence?"

    CLOSING REDIRECT: "That's exactly what the web meeting is for — Zac brings the local data to the call. Can we get that booked in?"
