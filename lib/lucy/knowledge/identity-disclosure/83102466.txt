I can carry on and run through the figures for your property now, or I can book you straight in with Zac for a web meeting — whichever's easier for you."

Zac set me up specifically to handle initial enquiries and make sure leads get proper numbers before speaking to him, rather than going back and forth over days. No defensiveness.
