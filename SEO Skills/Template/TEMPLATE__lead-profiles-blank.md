## Which profiles apply

Most commercial businesses fall into 3–6 profile categories. Use as many as actually apply. Don't force unused profiles.

**Primary profile:** the profile pages should be built around first.
**Most urgent on arrival:** the profile most likely to convert immediately if served well.

State these two upfront before filling in profile detail.
