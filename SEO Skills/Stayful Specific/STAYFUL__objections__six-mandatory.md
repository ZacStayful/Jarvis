# Stayful — Six Mandatory Objections

**Scope: Stayful Airbnb-management overlay.** Six objections that MUST be answered on every Stayful page. The first three must appear in body copy, not buried in the FAQ.

Universal framework: `CORE__04_frameworks__objections-and-faq-language-framework.md`.

---

## Objection 1 — "What happens in the slow months?"

**Class: Critical — body copy required.**

Address directly in body copy. Show monthly or seasonal variation honestly. Always show the worst-case annual net figure alongside the typical figure and compare it explicitly to the long-let equivalent.

**Never present peak projections without a full-year or worst-case figure alongside them.**

Full framing rules: `STAYFUL__objections__worst-case-framing-language.md`

---

## Objection 2 — "Can you guarantee the income?"

**Class: Critical — body copy near CTA required.**

Acknowledge directly that no STL provider — including Stayful — can guarantee a specific income figure. Position this honesty as a sign of credibility.

### Exact language to use near the income estimate form

> "We don't guarantee a fixed income figure — and we'd be cautious of any company that does. What we show you is the realistic range, including quieter months, based on comparable properties in your postcode. Even in a slower year, the net figure typically exceeds what a long-term tenancy would pay."

This statement is non-negotiable. Use exactly as worded.

---

## Objection 3 — "Do I lose control of my property?"

**Class: Critical — body copy near CTA required.**

### Exact language to use

> "You block dates you want to use the property in your owner calendar — no notice required, no approval process. And unlike a long-term tenancy, no guest has exclusive possession of your property."

Place near the income estimate form. Use exactly as worded.

---

## Objection 4 — "What if the figures aren't accurate?"

**Class: Standard — FAQ acceptable.**

Position the income estimate as honest and conservative — never best-case. Reference Stayful's own managed portfolio data. Acknowledge that no projection is a guarantee — then explain the 40% direct booking figure as the mechanism that reduces variability.

---

## Objection 5 — "What's the total fee load?"

**Class: Standard — FAQ acceptable.**

Address proactively before the reader works it out. Direct to the form: "The income estimate shows you the net figure."

---

## Objection 6 — "What about bad guests and property damage?"

**Class: Standard — FAQ acceptable.**

State the £100,000 insurance cover, the £200 security deposit and the ID check process clearly and early.

## Why these six are mandatory

These six are the objections every Profile B reader has, whether or not they ask out loud. Pages that don't address them lose Profile B conversions silently — the reader simply doesn't act.

Skipping any of the six is the single most common conversion-killing mistake on Stayful pages.

---

## The objection cascade — order matters

Objections come up in a sequence in the reader's mind. The page should address them in approximately this order:

1. **Slow months** (Zone 1 / 2 — alongside the income figure)
2. **Figure accuracy** (Zone 2 — when the reader starts asking "how do I know")
3. **Fee load** (Zone 2 — when total cost becomes a question)
4. **Bad guests** (Zone 3 — once the reader is considering acting)
5. **Guarantee** (Zone 3 near CTA — the final risk question)
6. **Control** (Zone 3 near CTA — the final friction question)

The cascade matches the three-zone architecture (`CORE__03_reader-momentum__escalating-stakes-and-curiosity-gap.md`). Each objection answers what the previous zone or section naturally raised.

---

## Common failures

### Failure 1 — Objections 2 and 3 in FAQ only

The most common Stayful page failure. Critical-class objections must be in body copy near the CTA. Putting them in FAQ means the reader has to actively look for them — and they often don't.

### Failure 2 — Worst case (Objection 1) only mentioned once

The slow-month figure should be referenced at least twice: alongside the headline income figure, and in the case study block. Single mentions get missed.

### Failure 3 — Objections answered with deflection

Objection 5 (fee load) answered with "Our pricing is competitive — see our pricing page" is deflection. Answer directly: "15% + VAT plus operating costs (cleaning, platform fees, supplies). The estimate shows you the net figure after all of these."

### Failure 4 — Wrong exact wording on Objection 2 or 3

The exact language above is non-negotiable. Paraphrases lose specific trust-signal elements (e.g. the warning about competitors who DO guarantee in Objection 2, the comparison to long-term tenancy in Objection 3).
