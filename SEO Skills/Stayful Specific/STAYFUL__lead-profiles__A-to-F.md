# Stayful — Lead Profiles A to F

**Scope: Stayful Airbnb-management overlay.** Six unified profiles every page should serve simultaneously. Universal framework: `CORE__04_frameworks__lead-profiles-framework.md`.

**Primary profile is B.** Most urgent on arrival is A. Profiles D, E, and F are low-conversion — the page still serves them but should not be written primarily for them.

---

## Profile A — Already running short lets, looking for a management company

Existing STL operator, often a single property. Frustrated and urgent — has an income problem costing money every month.

**Search intent:**
- "short let management company [city]"
- "switch short let management company"

**What the page must show them:**
- Proof of structured communication
- Performance better than market average
- Team structure
- Speed of onboarding

**Copy tone:** Direct and credible. No reassurance needed on whether STL works. Focus on what distinguishes Stayful's management approach.

**Key proof point:** 40% of bookings are direct — reduces income instability they may have experienced.

---

## Profile B — Existing landlord considering a switch from long-term letting

Currently on an AST, either tenant in situ has given notice or landlord is deciding whether to give notice.

**Primary organic lead segment — all pages built around this profile first.**

Two emotional entry points:

- **Rational evaluator** — analytically comparing net income
- **Frustration-driven** — emotionally motivated by poor tenancy experience

**Search intent:**
- "can I make more money short letting my property"
- "short term letting vs long term letting income"

**Emotional state:** Guarded and nervous. Significant financial decision. Will retreat to safety of doing nothing if trust is not built carefully.

**What the page must show them:**
- Honest income comparisons including slow months
- Clear explanation of what STL involves
- Reassurance on property control
- Proof that income floor on comparable properties beats the long-term alternative

**Copy tone:** Calm, honest, specific. Never hype. Never urgency tactics.

**Key copy priority:** Move this profile from income certainty questions toward service and process questions.

---

## Profile C — Considering purchasing a property specifically for short-term letting

First or second-time landlord validating income before completing a purchase.

**Search intent:**
- "short term let income estimate [city]"
- "Airbnb income [city] property"

**Emotional state:** Optimistic and research-oriented. Lower risk aversion than B. Often carry unrealistic income expectations from developer projections.

**What the page must show them:**
- Realistic net income figures — not gross
- Honest acknowledgement that developer cashflow figures often omit servicing costs
- Offer of property-specific viability assessment

**Copy tone:** Encouraging but grounding.

---

## Profile D — Selling a property, considering short-term letting in the meantime

Impatient. Their priority is the sale, not the STL arrangement.

**What the page must show them:**
- Honest acknowledgement that residential mortgage on a property being sold significantly limits profitability

**Copy tone:** Honest and efficient. Brief FAQ answer is sufficient.

---

## Profile E — Moving abroad, planning to short-let their home

Aspirational. Vision is location freedom. Residential mortgage makes profitability near-impossible.

**What the page must show them:**
- Honest, early statement that residential mortgages typically prevent STL from being profitable

**Copy tone:** Respectful of their vision. Acknowledge the goal, then give them honest information.

---

## Profile F — Rent-to-rent operator seeking management

Margin is structurally destroyed by combination of landlord rent and Stayful's management fee. FAQ note is sufficient.

---

## Profile modifier — Portfolio landlord

A portfolio landlord (two or more properties) can fall into any of the above profiles but converts more easily.

When writing copy that references "landlords" generally, the natural language of the portfolio landlord (commercially oriented, financially literate) should be the default register.

---

## How to use profiles in page strategy

### Matching page type to primary profile

| Page type | Primary profile | Secondary profiles served |
|---|---|---|
| City management pages | B | A and C through FAQ |
| Income estimate landing pages | C | B through worst-case framing |
| Management company comparison pages | A | B through performance data |
| Guides and supporting pages | Match guide topic to profile intent | — |

### H1 and H2 language by profile

**Profile A:**
- "Short let management company in [City] — what Stayful handles"
- "Why [City] short let landlords switch to Stayful"

**Profile B:**
- "Short-term letting in [City] — is it worth switching from a long-term tenancy?"
- "How much could you earn short-letting your [City] property?"

**Profile C:**
- "Short-term let income in [City] — what a property could realistically earn"
- "Buying a property to short let in [City] — honest income figures"
